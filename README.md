# Integrated Three-Statement Forecasting Model: Ferrari N.V. (NYSE: RACE)

📊 **An institutional-grade financial forecasting and valuation framework designed to stress-test the operational and capital structure of Ferrari N.V. over a 5-year horizon (2026–2030).**

---

## 🎯 Executive Overview
This project applies Wall Street investment banking standards (CFI/WSO methodology) to build a dynamic, fully integrated corporate finance model. Ferrari N.V. represents a unique modeling target due to its extreme luxury pricing power, heavy R&D capitalization for electrification, and bespoke capital expenditure cycles.

The model successfully connects the **Income Statement**, **Balance Sheet**, and **Cash Flow Statement** through an automated indirect cash-sweep loop, achieving a flawless balance check of zero across all historical and projected columns.

---

## ⚙️ Core Architecture & Features

*   **Dynamic Scenario Toggle Engine:** Built a centralized macroeconomic switch controlling 3 distinct operational cases (**Base Case, Upside Case, Downside Case**) that instantly recalibrates the entire model based on company guidance and market trends.
*   **Automated Balance Sheet Balancing:** Integrated a strict indirect Cash Flow Statement logic where future cash balances act as the structural "plug", resolving the Balance Check to exactly `0` under any chosen scenario.
*   **Bespoke Industrial Drivers:** Designed specific forecasting schedules for Working Capital (DSO, DIO, DPO), SG&A efficiency, and Capex/R&D intensity tailored to Maranello's exclusive supply chain and low-volume production strategy.
*   **Wall Street Formating Standards:** Implemented strict institutional presentation formatting, utilizing explicit color-coding (Blue for hardcoded historical inputs, Black for formulas) and clean layout guidelines (gridlines removed on introductory sheets).

---

## 🗺️ Repository & File Structure

*   📂 `Cover / Read Me` — Institutional cover page containing model metadata, ownership, and structural index.
*   📂 `Assumptions` — Centralized dashboard for macroeconomic variables, tax rates, and scenario percentages.
*   📂 `Income Statement` — 3-year historical analysis (2023-2025) and 5-year dynamic forecast of core operational profitability (EBITDA/EBIT) [Ferrari FY 2025 Financial Results].
*   📂 `Balance Sheet` — Full asset and liability mapping with integrated balance checks.
*   📂 `Cash Flow Statement` — Core cash generation engine tracking operational (CFO), investing (CFI), and financing (CFF) cash flows.
